# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Product Managers:** Collaborate on feature specs and acceptance criteria
- **Project Managers:** Provide status updates and effort estimates
- **QA/Testing Lead:** Work together on testability and defect resolution
- **Technical Lead/Architect:** Follow technical standards and design decisions

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Project Managers:** Partner on timeline and delivery planning
- **Developers:** Define requirements and acceptance criteria
- **Technical Lead/Architect:** Discuss feasibility and trade-offs
- **Stakeholder/Business Owner:** Align on business objectives and priorities

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Product Managers:** Align on priorities and scope
- **Developers:** Track progress and manage dependencies
- **QA/Testing Lead:** Monitor quality status and release readiness
- **Technical Lead/Architect:** Track technical risks and estimates
- **Stakeholder/Business Owner:** Escalate decisions and provide status

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define and execute quality strategy, manage test planning, validate acceptance criteria, and ensure quality standards are met throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Develop test plans and QA strategy aligned with project scope and risk profile
- Define acceptance criteria validation approaches (automated, manual, exploratory)
- Coordinate and execute testing activities (unit, integration, end-to-end, performance)
- Identify, triage, and track defects; prioritize fixes with the team
- Advise on quality risks and trade-offs between scope, schedule, and quality
- Establish Definition of Done quality criteria with the team
- Validate release readiness and coordinate post-deploy verification

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality features that meet acceptance criteria
- Reduce defect escape rate and post-release incidents
- Maintain fast feedback loops and quality signals throughout development

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement (QA input on testability)
- Daily standups (blockers, test status)
- PR reviews (test coverage and approach)
- Release readiness sign-off meetings

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers:** Collaborate on test coverage, testability of design, and defect triage
- **Product Managers:** Clarify acceptance criteria and prioritize testing focus
- **Project Managers:** Provide quality status, risks, and release readiness input
- **Technical Lead/Architect:** Collaborate on testability and quality approach

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads own technical design decisions, establish code quality and architectural standards, manage technical debt, and collaborate with product on feasibility, scalability, and performance considerations.

### Responsibilities
- Lead technical design and architecture discussions; document key decisions and rationale
- Establish and enforce code quality standards, coding conventions, and architectural patterns
- Review designs and major PRs for technical soundness and long-term maintainability
- Identify and prioritize technical debt; propose mitigation strategies
- Advise on feasibility, scalability, performance, and security implications of features
- Mentor developers and promote continuous technical improvement
- Collaborate with product on trade-offs between features and technical constraints

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and performant solutions
- Reduce technical debt and improve system health over time
- Enable fast, confident delivery through good architecture and standards

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code reviews and pair programming sessions
- Sprint planning (feasibility and effort estimates)
- Weekly alignment with Product Manager on trade-offs

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers:** Lead design discussions, mentor on standards, and review code
- **Product Managers:** Advise on feasibility and trade-offs; collaborate on scope decisions
- **Project Managers:** Provide technical risk assessment and effort estimates
- **QA/Testing Lead:** Collaborate on testability and quality approach

---

## Stakeholder / Business Owner

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Business Owners provide business context, make decisions on scope and priority, allocate resources, and ensure alignment between project delivery and business objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives, success metrics, and acceptance criteria (with Product Manager)
- Approve scope changes, trade-offs, and resource requests
- Make priority decisions when multiple initiatives compete for resources
- Provide regular feedback and sponsorship to the project
- Communicate project outcomes and impact to wider leadership
- Participate in key gates (initiation, planning, release) and decision-making
- Address escalations and unblock organizational dependencies

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Maintain alignment between project delivery and strategic priorities
- Reduce scope creep and decision delays through clear ownership

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and kickoff meetings
- Monthly or milestone-based stakeholder updates
- Go/no-go decisions and scope approval gates
- Ad-hoc escalations and trade-off discussions

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Product Managers:** Partner on business objectives, success metrics, and roadmap prioritization
- **Project Managers:** Receive status updates and escalations; make approval decisions
- **Developers:** Provide context and feedback through demos and reviews
- **QA/Testing Lead:** Validate quality and release readiness against business expectations
